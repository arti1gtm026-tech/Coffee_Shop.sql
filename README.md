# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis using SQL

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing coffee shop sales data using Microsoft SQL Server to uncover business insights related to sales performance, customer behavior, product demand, and market opportunities across different cities.

The analysis uses multiple datasets, including city, [customers (1)], product, and sales information, to evaluate revenue trends, identify top-selling products, understand customer distribution, and assess potential locations for coffee shop expansion.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze estimated coffee consumers across different cities.
- Calculate total revenue generated from coffee sales.
- Identify the most popular and top-selling coffee products.
- Evaluate average sales revenue per customer in each city.
- Compare city population with estimated coffee consumers.
- Analyze customer distribution across cities.
- Compare average sales per customer with estimated rent.
- Calculate monthly sales growth rates.
- Identify cities with the highest market potential.

## ❓ Business Questions

1. **Coffee Consumers Count:** How many people in each city are estimated to consume coffee, assuming 25% of the population consumes coffee?
2. **Total Revenue from Coffee Sales:** What is the total revenue generated from coffee sales across all cities in the last quarter of 2023?
3. **Sales Count for Each Product:** How many sales transactions have been recorded for each coffee product?
4. **Average Sales Amount per City:** What is the average sales amount per customer in each city?
5. **City Population and Coffee Consumers:** What are the population figures and estimated coffee consumers for each city?
6. **Top Selling Products by City:** What are the top three selling products in each city based on sales volume?
7. **Customer Segmentation by City:** How many unique customers have purchased coffee products in each city?
8. **Average Sale vs Rent:** How does average sales revenue per customer compare with estimated rent across different cities?
9. **Monthly Sales Growth:** What is the monthly sales growth or decline rate across different cities?
10. **Market Potential Analysis:** Which are the top three cities based on total sales, estimated rent, customer count, and potential coffee consumers?
